Herbs Omlete

Description

Herbs Omlete is an easy to cook Iranian dish. This dish is very healthy with fresh herbs and eggs and very tasty.

It is a very popular Iranian quick and light meal. It can be served with bread or rice.

Ingredients

  • 100g of herbs (chives, parsley and coriander)
  • 1 egg
  • Quarter of a tea spoon turmeric ground
  • 1 table spoon of oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Barberries and walnuts (optional)

Steps

  1. Chop herbs finely and add them to a mixing bowl.
  2. Add egg, turmeric ground, salt and pepper (and barberries and walnuts) to mixing bowl and mix them well.
  3. Heat up oil in a frying pan and add the mixture to the pan.
  4. Put on the lid and let it fry on one side for 7-8 minutes.
  5. Flip your omlete and put the lid on to fry on the other side for 5 minutes.
  6. Serve in a plate with your choice of bread or rice.
